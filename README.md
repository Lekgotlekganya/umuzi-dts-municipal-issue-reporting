# Community Connect
### Connecting Communities. Making Voices Heard.

**Community Connect** is a collaborative digital transformation project developed during the Umuzi Digital Transformation Sprint (DTS).

The project explores how technology can help bridge the communication gap between residents and their communities by making it easier to report local issues, track progress and discover community opportunities.

---

## The Problem

Many residents want to participate in improving their communities but encounter challenges when trying to access information, report problems or receive feedback.

Our team identified three key challenges:

- **Fragmented reporting:** Residents use different channels to report community issues, without a clear path to resolution.
- **Limited feedback:** Residents are often uncertain whether their reports have been received or addressed.
- **Scattered information:** Community opportunities, youth programmes, volunteering initiatives and local updates are not easily accessible in one place.

The challenge was not a lack of willingness to participate, but a lack of connection.

## Research & Discovery

Our team engaged with **35 residents** to better understand their needs, experiences and expectations.

### Key Findings

| Research finding | Percentage |
|---|---:|
| Residents who do not know what opportunities exist | 62.5% |
| Residents interested in youth programmes | 81.3% |
| Residents interested in volunteering | 59.4% |
| Residents who want proof that their reports were received and acted on | 53.1% |

These findings highlighted the importance of accessible information, communication and opportunities for community participation.

## The Proposed Solution

Community Connect was designed as a central hub where residents could connect with local services and opportunities.

### Core Features

**1. Report**
- Submit community and municipal issues.
- Receive a reference number for submitted reports.

**2. Track**
- Follow the progress of reported issues.
- View statuses such as *In Progress* and *Resolved*.

**3. Discover**
- Explore local businesses.
- Find volunteering opportunities and youth programmes.
- Access local community updates.

The concept aims to create a more connected experience between residents and the services and opportunities available to them.
